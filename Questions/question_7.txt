In a large governmental subcommittee building, there are three committee members who need to reach the central meeting room. Member A starts from the ecological research wing (6 blocks north, 4 blocks east of the entrance), Member B begins at the medications approval office (3 blocks north, 8 blocks west of the entrance), and Member C is at the amendments filing department (2 blocks south, 5 blocks east of the entrance). If each member can only move one block at a time (north, south, east, or west) and must avoid crossing paths with other members at any point during their journey to the central meeting room (located 1 block north of the entrance), what is the minimum total number of blocks that all three members must collectively travel to reach their destination without ever occupying the same block at the same time, even temporarily?
