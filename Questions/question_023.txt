Finally, you walk to Theodore Roosevelt's exhibit, which is directly west of Lincoln's and directly north of Washington's.

a) The length of the path from Jefferson to Lincoln in feet
b) The total area of the square formed by the four presidential exhibits in square feet
c) If you start at the entrance and visit all exhibits exactly once, returning to the entrance, what is the shortest possible path length in feet?
