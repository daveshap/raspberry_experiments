In a medical research facility, three immunology colleagues need to coordinate their movements during a September meditation break. The facility has a square layout where each side is 7 units long, with the main entrance at the southwest corner (0,0). Dr. Chen starts at the immunology lab (3,5) and walks 2 units south, then 3 units east. Dr. Patel begins at the meditation room (6,6) and moves 4 units west, then 2 units south. Dr. Rodriguez starts at the animation studio (1,2) and moves 3 units north, then 2 units east. If all three colleagues need to meet at a single point that minimizes their total remaining walking distance (moving only horizontally and vertically), at which coordinates should they meet, and what is their combined remaining walking distance to reach this optimal meeting point?
