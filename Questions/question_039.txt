A multinational electronics manufacturer needs to design a partitioned power supply circuit for consumers in three different countries. The input voltage varies by country: 120V (Country A), 230V (Country B), and 100V (Country C), all at 50Hz. The circuit must deliver exactly 12V DC output with a maximum power rating of 60W and maintain 85% minimum efficiency to meet international certification standards. Calculate:

1. The minimum transformer current rating needed for each country's version
2. The power loss in watts for each variant at full load
3. The maximum permissible resistance in the secondary circuit to maintain the required efficiency
4. The minimum capacitance needed in the smoothing circuit to keep voltage ripple below 1%

Express all answers in standard SI units and show which country's version poses the most challenging design constraints.
