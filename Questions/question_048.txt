A museum is planning four astronomy exhibitions over four consecutive months (January to April). The exhibitions are: "Solar System Wonders", "Deep Space Objects", "History of Telescopes", and "Cosmic Phenomena". The administrator must assign one curator (Amy, Bob, Carol, and David) to each exhibition following these rules:

1. Amy specializes in historical instruments and cannot work in January
2. Bob must manage an exhibition that occurs in a month adjacent to Carol's exhibition
3. David is allergic to the cleaning solutions used for telescope lenses and cannot manage "History of Telescopes"
4. The "Deep Space Objects" exhibition must occur before "Solar System Wonders"
5. Carol has a conference in April and cannot work that month

Determine the complete schedule of who manages which exhibition in which month.
