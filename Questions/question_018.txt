A hospital's infection control department is reviewing four professional cases that occurred on different days (Monday through Thursday). For each case, there was a specific infection type (bacterial, viral, fungal, or prion), and a distinct metabolism-related symptom (fever, fatigue, loss of appetite, or nausea). Given these conditions:

1. The bacterial case was handled two days after the case with fever symptoms.
2. The Thursday case involved neither prion infection nor nausea.
3. The fungal infection case immediately followed the case with fatigue symptoms.
4. The case with loss of appetite occurred on Monday.
5. The viral infection case was not associated with fever symptoms.
6. The case with nausea occurred on Wednesday.

Determine the complete and correct reconstruction of each day's case, including the infection type and corresponding symptom.
