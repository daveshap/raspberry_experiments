A customer service center handles four types of enquiries: Technical Support, Product Information, Order Confirmation, and Volunteer Coordination. Four employees (Alice, Bob, Carol, and David) need to be assigned to maintain these services while satisfying these conditions:

1. The person handling Technical Support must also be trained in telephony systems
2. Only Bob and Carol are trained in telephony
3. Alice must be assigned to a service that requires less technical skill than David's assignment
4. The person handling Volunteer Coordination must be adjacent (in desk position) to the person handling Order Confirmation
5. Carol cannot sit next to Bob for personal reasons
6. Desk positions are numbered 1-4 from left to right
7. The Product Information desk must be position 2 or 3

Determine the complete arrangement of who handles which service and sits at which desk position.
