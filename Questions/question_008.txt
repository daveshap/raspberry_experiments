In Luxembourg's Ministry of Transport, four officials (A, B, C, and D) must be assigned to different floors (1-4) of their building for a motorcycle safety inspection program. The following conditions must be met:

1. Official A must be exactly two floors above Official B
2. Official C cannot be on the highest or lowest floor
3. Official D must be on an even-numbered floor
4. The floor number of Official C must be equal to the absolute difference between A's and D's floor numbers
5. B cannot be on Floor 1

Determine the unique valid arrangement of officials across the four floors.
