A specialist environmental scientist in Newcastle is studying air pollution particles, comparing them with similar data from Springfield. Given that:
- The average PM2.5 particle concentration in Newcastle is 12 µg/m³
- The wind speed averages 15 mph from Newcastle toward Springfield
- The distance between cities is 200 miles
- The particle decay rate is 2% per hour in normal conditions

Calculate how long it would take for these particles to reach Springfield, and what the final concentration would be. Then explain whether this level would be considered desirable from a sustainability perspective, citing typical WHO air quality guidelines in your reasoning. Consider both atmospheric dispersion and decay rates in your calculations.
