A computer store has two processors on sale. The first processor costs $240 and performs 3.2 billion calculations per second, while consuming 95 watts of power. The second processor costs $180 and performs 2.8 billion calculations per second, while consuming 65 watts of power. If electricity costs $0.12 per kilowatt-hour, and both processors will run continuously for one year, which processor is more cost-efficient in terms of total cost (purchase price plus electricity cost) per billion calculations performed?
