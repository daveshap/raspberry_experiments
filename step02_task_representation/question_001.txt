<task_representation>
<problem_statement>
Calculate the total second-quarter revenue for three retail stores given:
- First store Q1 revenue was $120,000
- Second and third stores generate 15% less than the previous store in Q1
- All stores increased Q2 revenue by 8% from their Q1 amounts
- Each store lost 3 sales days in Q2 due to connectivity issues
- Each store's daily revenue is consistent throughout the quarter
- A quarter consists of 91 days
</problem_statement>

<constraints>
1. Revenue decreases by 15% for each subsequent store in Q1
2. Q2 revenue represents an 8% increase from Q1 base amounts
3. Each store loses exactly 3 operating days in Q2
4. Daily revenue must be calculated based on 91 days per quarter
5. Daily revenue is assumed constant throughout the quarter for each store
6. Revenue calculations must account for both the increase and the lost days
</constraints>

<knowns>
1. Store 1 Q1 revenue: $120,000
2. Store revenue reduction rate: 15% per subsequent store
3. Q2 growth rate: 8%
4. Days in a quarter: 91
5. Lost days per store: 3
6. Operating days in Q2: 88 (91 - 3)
</constraints>

<unknowns>
1. Store 2 Q1 revenue
2. Store 3 Q1 revenue
3. Each store's Q2 base revenue (before lost days)
4. Daily revenue rate for each store in Q2
5. Final Q2 revenue for each store
6. Total combined Q2 revenue
</unknowns>

<scope>
Included:
- Revenue calculations for all three stores
- Impact of lost operating days
- Sequential revenue reductions
- Quarter-over-quarter growth

Assumptions:
- Revenue is distributed evenly across all operating days
- The 8% Q2 increase is calculated before accounting for lost days
- All stores remain operational except for the specified 3 lost days
- Revenue reductions between stores remain constant
</scope>
</task_representation>
