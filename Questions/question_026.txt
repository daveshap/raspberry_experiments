During a routine inspection, you discover that a climate-controlled storage room containing sensitive biological samples has experienced a power failure. The walls are made of standard laboratory-grade insulated panels.

Calculate the approximate current temperature in the room and determine if the biological samples (which must be kept below 8°C) are still viable. Show your reasoning and calculations, considering:
- Heat transfer through walls
- The thermal mass of the air
- The insulation properties of standard laboratory walls
- The starting and ending conditions
