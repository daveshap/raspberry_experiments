Three furniture store owners in a small town face a strategic decision during a natural disaster. Each owner receives a daily newsletter about refugee needs and must decide whether to share their inventory information with others. The payoff structure is as follows:

- If all three share information, each gets 10 units of profit
- If two share and one withholds, sharing owners get 5 units each, withholding owner gets 15 units
- If one shares and two withhold, sharing owner gets 0 units, withholding owners get 12 units each
- If no one shares, each gets 6 units

Additionally:
- Sharing owners must fulfill obligations to supply emergency furniture to survivors
- Newsletter information becomes public next day regardless of choices
- Each owner must make this decision for 3 consecutive days

Analyze:
1. Find all pure strategy Nash equilibria for a single day
2. Determine if these equilibria change in the 3-day repeated game
3. Identify the socially optimal strategy and explain why it might differ from the Nash equilibria
4. Calculate the total community welfare under each possible outcome
