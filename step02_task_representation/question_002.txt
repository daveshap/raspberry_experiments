<task_representation>
<problem_statement>
Given five suspects interviewed on consecutive days (Monday through Friday) and a set of logical conditions regarding their residences (house or apartment) and potential entry points (garage door or basement window), we must determine:
1. Which entry point was used in the break-in
2. Which suspect (identified by their interview day) is the defendant
</problem_statement>

<constraints>
1. Each suspect was interviewed on exactly one day from Monday through Friday
2. Each suspect must live in either a house or an apartment
3. The entry point must be either the garage door or basement window
4. The defendant was interviewed on Friday
5. The Wednesday interviewee lives in an apartment
6. The garage door suspect's interview occurred before the house-dwelling suspect's interview
7. If entry was through basement window, then Tuesday's suspect is innocent
8. If Monday's suspect lives in a house, then the garage door was used
</constraints>

<knowns>
1. There are exactly five suspects
2. Interview days are Monday through Friday (sequential)
3. The defendant was interviewed on Friday
4. Wednesday's suspect lives in an apartment
5. Two possible entry points: garage door or basement window
6. Two possible residence types: house or apartment
7. Written instructions were left at the scene by the defendant
</knowns>

<unknowns>
1. The residence type (house/apartment) for all suspects except Wednesday's
2. The actual entry point used
3. The residence type of the defendant (Friday's suspect)
4. The complete sequence of which suspects live in houses vs apartments
</unknowns>

<scope>
Included:
- Logical relationships between interview days, residence types, and entry points
- Binary conditions (house/apartment, garage/window)
- Temporal sequence of interviews

Not included:
- Physical evidence beyond the written instructions
- Other potential entry points
- Other suspects beyond the five interviewed
- Details about the written instructions
- Time of day for interviews or break-in

Assumptions permitted:
- All given information is accurate and complete
- Each suspect must be either guilty or innocent
- The defendant is among the five suspects interviewed
- Only one entry point was used
</scope>
</task_representation>
